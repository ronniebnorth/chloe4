### India / raga: planned and researched, no code yet
- Ronnie: "Are we missing an India journey? … yes we need to do that."
- `docs/IDEA_RAGA.md` (commits `73ebb54`, `00919ca`, pushed) has the plan,
  the raga table, the tuning research and the build order.
- **The research finding that shapes the build:** a raga's special notes
  are *shapes* (how the note is approached, how it swings and how it
  leaves), not fixed pitches (van der Meer & Rao). Measured values exist
  for Todi (Bel: r 95, g 294, M ~600, d 792 upper / 806 lower, N 1107) and
  for komal Dha in five ragas (~804–819¢, so Bhairav's and Darbari's Dha are
  NOT the ati-komal 792 the textbooks claim). No measured data was found for
  Yaman, Bhimpalasi, Bageshri or Malkauns, so they start from just
  intonation. Darbari's Ga has no fixed pitch, and its centre is Ronnie's
  call by ear.
