## What Chloe4 is now (built 2026-11-08 → 09)

- **Pitch in cents.** Grid scales are 24-bit root-first codes (one bit per
  quarter tone); everything else is an exact list of cents with a stable ID
  hashed from its pitches (`fromCents` in `src/scale24.js`). All 4,095
  Chloe3 scales verified identical.
- **World scales:** maqam and dastgah (24-step, half-flats written E½♭),
  Turkish makam (53 commas, B⁻¹), real intonation in cents (Just major/modes,
  Pythagorean, overtone scale, Shur as played; E−14¢). Plus 9–12 note outside
  scales.
- **Journeys:** persia (dastgah tour), shur (one dastgah, home note climbs),
  makam (Turkish, darbuka usuls incl. Aksak 9/8 and Curcuna 10/8). Draw
  journey has a scales row (all / western / maqam · dastgah / turkish / real
  intonation).
- **Studio controls added:** RESOLUTION (12–1200 steps per octave, rounds
  playback); BRIGHT FEEL (root→felt slider: note memory weights held notes;
  wander); MOOD (chill↔epic: tempo, length, energy, reverb, sounds, tension;
  wander); WORLD SCALES (share of picks from world groups; natural/0–100%;
  wander); orchestra wander; NOTES number buttons 3–12 + all; makam beat.
  Every wander has speed and range sliders.
- **Fixes:** right panel scrolls as a whole; button rows wrap; instrument
  row shows the sound a journey/world is playing (dashed); journeys raise
  RESOLUTION they need (makam ≥ 53, persia/shur ≥ 24).
- **Voices (added later on 11-09):** three new instruments. `choir` (sampled GM
  choir "aah", loop points) and `oohs` (sampled "ooh") are loaded on first
  use, and chant stands in until they arrive. `chant` is a formant synth: 3 saw
  voices, a vowel that follows MOOD, scoops and glides. New drone type `ison`:
  4 low men, each breathing on his own. The byzantine journey sings chant ↔ choir
  over the ison. The orchestra adds the sampled choir on chords toward the epic end
  of MOOD. Levels measured offline: within ~1 dB of piano/duduk. The voice samples
  are about 20 dB quiet, so their output gain is ×10.
- Links carry everything: e.g. world-scales wander, exact pitches, outside
  on, all sizes: `?view=studio&wsw=1&edo=1200&xo=1&nr=3-12`.

## Listen and judge (nothing here has been heard by Ronnie yet unless he says so)

1. Do the half-flats (persia, shur) and comma notes (makam) sound like real
   Persian/Turkish music or just out of tune?
2. The darbuka usuls: do they feel right? (Simplified common renderings.)
3. Felt vs root brightness: audible difference? Best with drone off. Tune
   the 3 s memory fade, drone weight 1.5, felt strength 0.3 in App.jsx.
4. MOOD strengths: tempo ±18%, energy ±0.35. Too much / too little?
5. Orchestra wander: are the comings and goings smooth or abrupt? (Longer
   fades would be the fix.)
6. Voices: does chant sound like singing or like a synth? The vowel
   formants and vibrato (5–6 Hz, 10–20¢) are the knobs. Ison: too buzzy?
   Is the choir in the orchestra too loud at full epic (choirG 0.35)?
7. Just modes have a "wolf" 5th (Just Dorian 680¢): keep, or hide those modes?
