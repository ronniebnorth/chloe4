## What Chloe4 is now

### Core (built 11-08 → 09)
- **Pitch in cents.** Grid scales are 24-bit root-first codes (one bit per
  quarter tone). Everything else is an exact list of cents with a stable ID
  hashed from its pitches (`fromCents` in `src/scale24.js`). All 4,095 Chloe3
  scales are verified identical.
- **World scales:**
  - maqam and dastgah (24-step, half-flats written E½♭);
  - Turkish makam (53 commas, B⁻¹);
  - Byzantine (72 moria);
  - real intonation in cents (E−14¢): Just major and its modes, Pythagorean, the
    overtone scale, Shur as played, the **Chinese pentatonic and old
    heptatonic** in pure fifths, and **gamelan slendro and pelog** (bem, barang,
    all seven).
  - 9–12 note outside scales.
- **Studio controls:**
  - RESOLUTION (12–1200 steps per octave);
  - BRIGHT FEEL (root ↔ felt, with note memory);
  - MOOD (chill ↔ epic);
  - WORLD SCALES (natural / 0–100%);
  - orchestra wander;
  - NOTES buttons (3–12 + all).

  Every wander has speed and range sliders.

### Added on 11-09
- **Key in cents.** `keyCents` is folded into the effective concert pitch
  (`aRef = aRefRaw · 2^(keyCents/1200)`), so everything follows. Journeys set it
  through fractional station `shift`; stopping a journey resets it. The root
  shows as D½♭ or F+22¢. Brought back **Abu Ata** in the Shur journey; the makam
  journey's **Dügah** is now exactly 9 commas (203.8¢) above Rast.
- **Spiral journey** (Qian Lezhi / Jing Fang). The Chinese pentatonic in pure
  fifths, the home note climbing a pure fifth per move: 53 moves over the
  journey, then on along Qian's chain toward 360. The key drifts (+23.5¢ after 12
  moves). It shows "(fifth k of 360)". The lead rotates dulcimer → flute → tuned
  gong. The **opera gong** marks each move, and the **tam-tam** sounds on passing
  every 12th move. Moves happen at Auto's picks, not on a fixed clock.
- **Voices:**
  - `choir` and `oohs` are sampled GM voices (loaded on first use; chant stands
    in until then);
  - `chant` is a formant synth whose vowel follows MOOD;
  - the drone type `ison` is 4 low men breathing at different times.

  The byzantine journey sings chant ↔ choir over the ison, and the orchestra adds
  a choir toward epic MOOD.
- **Gongs:**
  - `gong`: a tuned bossed gong;
  - `tamtam`: the big Chinese gong, 129 partials rendered once in an
    OfflineAudioContext (bases 65/130/260 Hz, ~2.5 s to render); the tuned gong
    stands in until it's ready, and more than 4 rings damp the oldest;
  - `opera`: daluo below G3 slides down, xiaoluo above slides up;
  - `saron`: a gamelan bronze bar.
- **Tam-tam accents** (`strikeTam`, ≥ 20 s apart):
  - Storm and Rise peaks (`journeyPeak`);
  - orchestra resolutions and the march's last stab;
  - the temple world every 2–4 min;
  - every 12th spiral move.
- **Gamelan world** (`wo=gamelan`, beat `gamelan`):
  - bonang (tuned gong) lead;
  - in lancaran form: a 16-beat gongan with a generated **balungan** (repeats
    2–3×) played by the **saron**, the **peking** an octave up (nacah-like);
  - kethuk, kempul, kenong and gong ageng on the balungan's notes;
  - a synthesised **kendhang** (dhung / tung / tak / dhang) with a fill into
    each gong;
  - **irama**: calm → dadi (balungan half speed, twice as dense around it),
    tension → lancar, changing only at the gong after a kendhang fill.

## Listen and judge (nothing here has been heard by Ronnie unless he says so)

1. Half-flats (persia, shur) and commas (makam): real, or out of tune?
2. Darbuka usuls: do they feel right? (Simplified common renderings.)
3. Felt vs root brightness: audible? Knobs: 3 s memory fade, drone weight 1.5,
   felt strength 0.3.
4. MOOD strengths (tempo ±18%, energy ±0.35).
5. Orchestra wander: smooth or abrupt comings and goings?
6. Voices: does chant sound sung or synthy? (Formants, vibrato 5–6 Hz /
   10–20¢.) Ison too buzzy? Orchestra choir too loud at full epic (choirG 0.35)?
7. Just modes' "wolf" 5th (Just Dorian 680¢): keep or hide?
8. **Tuned gong:** Ronnie said "cool", though he'd meant a big gong
   (→ tam-tam). Knobs: ring length, beat speed, mallet, lands 7¢ sharp.
9. **Tam-tam:** is it the gong he hears in his head? Knobs: bloom time,
   shimmer brightness, ring length, mallet.
10. **Opera gong:** slide amounts (−160…−240¢ big, +120…+180¢ small) are by ear
    from descriptions.
11. **Accents:** too frequent, or too loud? (vel 0.28–0.32; temple 0.16.)
12. **Gamelan:** balance of saron / peking / gongs / kendhang; is irama dadi
    obvious? The kendhang patterns are Chloe's own, not traditional ones.
